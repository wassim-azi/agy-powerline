agy statusline themes

agy-powerline has {{count}} built-in themes for dark, light and color-blind terminals. Each one below is drawn by the real statusline code in the three layouts, with its lowest contrast figures and its ok, caution and danger colors. Switch with one command, or open a theme in the configurator.

Contrast figures are the lowest across all widget colors: "Pill text" is text on a pill, "On terminal" is the lines layout text on the intended terminal background. 4.5:1 is the WCAG AA minimum for body text. Pill caps take the terminal's own background, so they blend into any terminal. Browsers can't draw Nerd Font glyphs, so the round caps and arrows are drawn with CSS in the same colors.
